# Program brief: Product launch kit

Ship a paid feature launch with coordinated pricing, announcement copy, landing visuals, and a go-to-market campaign.

## Goals

1. Publish list price and packaging that finance owns and other teams consume.
2. Announce the feature in on-brand, sourced copy.
3. Lay out the landing hero and pricing section for engineering to implement.
4. Run a measured email + LinkedIn campaign that stays on the frozen positioning.

## Non-goals

- Building the full billing system in this program (engineering delivers announcement surface only).
- Paid media buying execution outside the planned channels.
